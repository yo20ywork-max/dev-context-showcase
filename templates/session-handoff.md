# Session Handoff Template

## Goal

State the concrete user outcome.

## Source and scope

Record the repository, branch, relevant component, and intended changes.

## Completed work

Describe what changed and its observable effect.

## Verification

Record commands or observations, results, and what they do not establish.

## Open items

List remaining work, blockers, and the next useful action.

## Publication boundary

Keep private account data, credentials, internal transcripts, and unpublished company material out of public copies.
