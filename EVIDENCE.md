# Public Method and Reusable Templates

The private development-context repository maintains project ownership, source/deployment relationships, durable decisions, and cross-machine handoff notes.

This public version contains newly written, sanitized templates that illustrate that workflow:

- [Project registry template](templates/project-registry.md)
- [Session handoff template](templates/session-handoff.md)

These are public examples of the method, not copies of the operational registry or team memory. No internal hostnames, machine paths, credentials, or private release records are included. This is supporting engineering process work rather than a separately shipped customer product.
